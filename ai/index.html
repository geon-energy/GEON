GEON Verification Levels

L0 Lead — raw opportunity or initial signal.
L1 Identity Verified — submitting entity identified.
L2 Resource Declared — structured resource information submitted.
L3 Documentation Verified — key evidence reviewed.
L4 Technical Qualified — sufficient technical information for preliminary assessment.
L5 Commercial Qualified — commercial conditions sufficiently developed.
L6 Project Ready — suitable for transaction or project execution.
Oman Pilot Market

GEON is initially testing its energy opportunity discovery and verification model in Oman, with a focus on reliable gas-based power, energy resources, infrastructure and large-scale digital infrastructure demand.
